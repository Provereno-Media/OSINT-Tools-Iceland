# OSINT-Tools-Iceland
<img src="https://flagcdn.com/w320/is.png" alt="Iceland_FLAG"/>
A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Iceland. Most of the resources and tools has versions in Icelandic, some also in English. Many government services have moved to the common portal Ísland.is, and lookups such as the vehicle owner search require Icelandic electronic ID (rafræn skilríki) through "Mínar síður". Persons and companies are identified by the kennitala (national ID number), which is the best search key in most registers. Iceland is a small jurisdiction, so several categories rely on international services. Enter names with Icelandic letters (á, ð, é, í, ó, ú, ý, þ, æ, ö).

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [Opin gögn (Iceland Open Data)](https://island.is/s/stafraent-island/opingogn) — Digital Iceland's page for open government data on Ísland.is. *Lang: IS, EN.*
- [Hagstofa Íslands — Talnagrunnur (Statistics Iceland — PX-Web Database)](https://px.hagstofa.is/pxis/pxweb/is/) — Statistics Iceland's database of official statistical tables, with downloads and an API. *Lang: IS, EN.*
- [Seðlabanki Íslands (Central Bank of Iceland)](https://sedlabanki.is/) — central bank and financial supervisor: interest and exchange rates, financial statistics and the register of supervised entities. *Lang: IS, EN.*
- [Stjórnartíðindi (Government Gazette)](https://island.is/stjornartidindi) — official publication of laws, regulations and government notices under Act no. 15/2005. *Lang: IS.*
- [Lögbirtingablaðið (Legal Gazette)](https://logbirtingablad.is/) — Ministry of Justice's legal gazette: bankruptcy and estate notices, summonses, company notices and compulsory auctions; all issues since 1 January 2001 online. *Lang: IS.*
- [Hæstiréttur Íslands — Dómaleit (Supreme Court — Judgment Search)](https://island.is/s/haestirettur) — Supreme Court judgments and decisions on leave to appeal, with legal summaries of selected cases in English. *Lang: IS, EN.*
- [Landsréttur (Court of Appeal)](https://island.is/s/landsrettur) — Court of Appeal pages on Ísland.is with its judgments and rulings. *Lang: IS.*
- [Héraðsdómstólar (District Courts)](https://island.is/s/heradsdomstolar) — District Courts' pages on Ísland.is with their judgments. *Lang: IS.*
- [Stjórnarráðið — Úrskurðir og álit (Government Offices — Rulings and Opinions)](https://www.stjornarradid.is/gogn/urskurdir-og-alit-/) — rulings and opinions of ministries and appeal committees. *Lang: IS.*

## Legal Entities
- [Fyrirtækjaskrá — Leit (Register of Enterprises Search)](https://www.skatturinn.is/fyrirtaekjaskra/leit) — Iceland Revenue and Customs' free search of the register of enterprises, the annual accounts register and the VAT register by name, address, kennitala or VAT number; shows newly registered companies. Extracts and annual accounts are paid. *Lang: IS.*
- [Creditinfo Ísland](https://www.creditinfo.is/) — credit-information company: company information, ownership and links, PEP checks, defaults and KYC. Paid. *Lang: IS, EN.*
- [Keldan](https://www.keldan.is/) — financial-market information on Iceland: share prices on Nasdaq Iceland and First North, and company data search. Subscription for most data. *Lang: IS.*
- [Tesari First Resort](https://tesari.ai/) — Tesari is an all-source intelligence platform designed for high-stakes environments, with applications ranging from trade compliance and supply chain illumination to financial crime investigations and complex litigation. It is a paid platform that offers a Tech for Good (Community Impact) Programme for OSINT practitioners and journalists. *Lang: EN.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [Fasteignamat og fasteignaskrá (Property Valuation and Property Register)](https://island.is/fasteignamat) — Housing and Construction Authority (HMS) information on property valuation; property register data (property numbers and details by address) is available through Ísland.is. *Lang: IS.*
- [Kortasjá — Kortagluggi (National Map Viewer)](https://kortagluggi.is/mapview/?app=kort) — national map viewer with topographic, aerial and thematic layers. *Lang: IS.*
- [Borgarvefsjá (Reykjavík City Map)](https://reykjavik.is/borgarvefsja) — free web GIS for Reykjavík and surroundings with 14 themes: districts, streets, buildings and plots, utilities, demographics and properties, cultural heritage, imagery. *Lang: IS.*
- [Já.is — Kort (Map with 360° Street View)](https://ja.is/kort/) — map of Iceland with search, directions and Já's own 360° street-level imagery. *Lang: IS.*

## Vehicles
### Land
- [Uppfletting í ökutækjaskrá (Vehicle Register Lookup)](https://island.is/uppfletting-i-oekutaekjaskra) — Icelandic Transport Authority lookup on Ísland.is: simple search gives basic vehicle data; the detailed search (5 free lookups per day, Icelandic e-ID login) also shows the registered owner. *Lang: IS.*

### Maritime
- [Skipaskrá (Ship Register)](https://island.is/skipaskra) — Icelandic Transport Authority's main ship register under the Ships Act no. 66/2021, published as a full list as of 1 January each year: registration and district numbers, call sign, former name, home port, builder, type, engine, tonnage and dimensions. *Lang: IS.*
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/) — live AIS vessel map with vessel and port database. Freemium: live map free; some features paid. *Lang: EN.*
- [Equasis](https://www.equasis.org/) — international database of ship and shipping-company safety information: IMO number, flag, owner, manager, inspections. Free registration required. *Lang: EN.*

### Aviation
- [Loftfaraskrá (Aircraft Register)](https://island.is/loftfaraskra) — aeroplanes and helicopters registered in Iceland; search by registration number or owner, with serial number and production year. *Lang: IS.*
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/) — live and historical aircraft tracking without filtering of blocked aircraft; search by registration, ICAO hex or callsign. *Lang: EN.*

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [Já.is](https://ja.is/) — national phone directory: persons and companies by name, phone number or address. *Lang: IS.*
- [Alþingi — Hagsmunaskráning þingmanna (Parliament — Register of MPs' Interests)](https://www.althingi.is/altext/cv/is/hagsmunaskra/) — MPs' self-declared financial interests and positions outside parliament, with registration dates. Blocks automated access. *Lang: IS.*
- [Hagstofa Íslands — Kosningar (Statistics Iceland — Elections)](https://hagstofa.is/talnaefni/ibuar/kosningar/) — official statistics and results of parliamentary, presidential and municipal elections and referendums. *Lang: IS, EN.*
- [Starfsleyfaskrá heilbrigðisstarfsfólks (Register of Health Professionals' Licences)](https://island.is/starfsleyfaskra) — public register of licensed health professionals on Ísland.is. *Lang: IS.*
- [Lögmannafélag Íslands — Félagatal (Icelandic Bar Association — Members)](https://www.lmfi.is/felagatal) — search of members of the Icelandic Bar Association. *Lang: IS, EN.*
- [Íslendingabók](https://www.islendingabok.is/) — genealogical database of nearly all Icelanders for whom sources exist; login with Icelandic e-ID required. *Lang: IS.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [Opnir reikningar ríkisins (Open Government Invoices)](https://www.opnirreikningar.is/) — paid invoices of state agencies: buyer, supplier, cost type, date and amount; filter and export. *Lang: IS.*
- [Útboðsvefur.is (Public Tender Web)](https://www.utbodsvefur.is/) — public tenders of state agencies, municipalities and public companies; filter by contracting authority. *Lang: IS.*
- [Fjársýslan (Financial Management Authority)](https://island.is/s/fjarsyslan) — state agency for public finances and central purchasing (formerly Ríkiskaup): framework agreements and procurement advice. *Lang: IS.*
- [TED — Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu/en/) — EU/EEA procurement-notice search; filter by country Iceland for above-threshold notices and contract awards. *Lang: EN, multi.*

## WHOIS
- [ISNIC — Leit í rétthafaskrá (WHOIS)](https://www.isnic.is/is/whois) — official .is registry WHOIS: holders of all Icelandic domains and their contacts; search by domain, NIC handle, name server or IP address. *Lang: IS, EN.*
- [IANA — .is Delegation Record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/is.html) — official delegation data for .is: registry operator ISNIC, contacts and WHOIS server `whois.isnic.is`. *Lang: EN.*
- [RIPE Database Query](https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query) — RIPE NCC WHOIS for IP ranges and AS numbers allocated to Icelandic networks. *Lang: EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records and subdomain discovery. *Lang: EN.*
